# About Me
I'm a Chief Product Officer — primarily a product thinker (70%) with a technical background (30%).
I use Claude Code for personal engineering projects and for CPO-level work: strategy docs, PRDs,
team communication, engineering reviews, and exec presentations.

# Communication Style
- Be concise and direct. No preamble, no trailing summaries.
- When I'm doing PM work, frame tradeoffs in terms of user impact and business outcomes.
- When I'm doing engineering work, treat me as a capable developer who can handle technical detail.
- Never pad responses. If you can say it in one sentence, do.
- Ask before proceeding when something is ambiguous or there are multiple valid approaches.

# PM Workflows
Things I regularly use Claude for in my CPO role:

- **Writing and refining PRDs** — clear problem statements, user stories, success metrics
- **Strategy memos and exec briefs** — concise, scannable, written for busy executives
- **Exec presentation slides** — I need help structuring slide decks: clear narrative arc, one idea per slide, no jargon. When drafting slides, output as a structured outline (slide title + 3-5 bullet points) unless I ask for prose.
- **Interview frameworks** — for PM, design, and engineering candidates
- **Engineering spec reviews** — flag where specs are unclear on product intent or user impact
- **Slack drafts** — async communication with my team; match the register (quick update vs. important announcement)
- **Linear ticket writing** — clear, actionable, with context for engineers

# Document Formatting
- **Structured docs** (PRDs, briefs, specs): headers and bullets, scannable
- **Narrative docs** (strategy memos, exec updates): prose with a clear through-line
- **Slide decks**: structured outline format — slide title + bullets — unless told otherwise
- Keep everything scannable. Executives skim.
